# Record Room — notes for Claude

Bobby's hobby project: a cozy lofi room you can play music in. A group of people (different for each room) hangs out watching the TV on the left wall, which plays the main broadcast (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rFZHOHl-L8A) as the room's radio. Records are the way to play other music: pick one from the crate and it floats to the turntable, the needle drops, and the song plays on the TV. Build in small steps and add features as we go.

Live site: https://lobbybane.github.io/record-room/ (GitHub Pages from `main`, root folder).

## Decisions already made

- **Look: lofi / anime night city.** Chosen over an earlier mid-century screen-print "park-service poster" version. Soft violet, pink and peach palette, a window onto a rainy city (neon signs, lit windows, elevated train), string lights, plants, a sleeping cat on the sill, a retro CRT TV on a console on the left wall, a lamp and a turntable on a cabinet. Grain and vignette on top. Keep new work in this style.
- **People, not a single listener (Nov 2026 redesign):** each room has a casual themed group facing the TV. No album covers on the walls (posters and record shelves were removed); records live in the crate. The listener's outfit/skin/activity settings were replaced by one `crowd` setting (busy / cozy / empty).
- **Music: YouTube's official IFrame Player API.** Free, full songs, no subscription. The player must stay visible (it lives on the TV screen). Never hide it, rip audio, download or re-host anything (no yt-dlp). Some uploads block embedding; show a friendly message and suggest another upload.
- **Covers: iTunes Search API** via JSONP, no key. Fallback is the YouTube thumbnail. Load covers at runtime, never save copies into the repo.
- **Ruled out for now:** Apple MusicKit (needs the $99/yr Apple Developer Program plus the listener's Apple Music subscription; Bobby has only a free developer account), Amazon Music (closed beta, approval and DRM required), Spotify (Premium plus tightened developer access). The player is written so another source could be swapped in later.
- **Stack:** plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript modules, no build step, no dependencies. Keep it that way unless there's a strong reason.

## How it works

- The scene is a fixed 1280 × 820 stage scaled to fit the window (`--s` CSS variable set in `js/main.js`). All positions are stage pixels.
- The whole room always stays visible. Spare screen space is filled by the wall, baseboard, floor, vignette and grain, which stretch to the screen edges using `--fx` / `--fy` / `--fw` / `--fh` (the screen's left, top, width and height in stage pixels). The stage centre is `--cx` / `--cy`. While the Decorate panel is open the room refits into the space beside it (or above it on phones). The Decorate button sits in the screen's top-right corner. The page is `position: fixed` so iPhone Safari's toolbar can't hide the bottom.
- `index.html` — the room markup and SVG shapes.
- `css/style.css` — styling and all animations (weather, city, pets, fish, people, holidays, record spin, tonearm), plus the reduced-motion fallbacks.
- `js/main.js` — fits the stage (wide layout, or the phone layout: room on top, `.tv-set` enlarged below via `--tv-t`, page scrolls with `#scroll-spacer`; the TV box is x 14–330 × y 192–446 and `.tv-ghost` stands in for it on the console on phones), builds the city, the crate, the record animation (Web Animations API), playback states (`data-phase` on the stage; `updateCard()` keeps the stage, marquee, `#deck-btn` and the screen-reader line `#np-status` in step — there is **no now-playing card on screen** (removed Nov 2026): the TV knobs change station and volume, YouTube's own controls pause, tapping the spinning record (`#deck-btn`) opens Put It Back / Skip / Remove from Crate, and in the theater tapping the marquee (`#th-next`) plays the next showing), the add-a-record dialog, and redraws the people when the room, crowd or season changes. The record on the turntable is `current = { from: 'crate', index }`; `recordOf()` / `originOf()` resolve it.
- The TV: `.tv-set` (screen 280 × 200 at x 32, y 207; knobs `#tv-knob` / `#tv-volume`) sits on `.tv-console` on the left wall, under `.wall-shelf` (fish tank, cassette, plant, the holiday tree). Notes drift off it while music plays (`.tv-fx`).
- `js/people.js` + `js/groups.js` — the people. `groups.js` describes each room's group as data (seat position, size, pose sit/floor/stand/recline/lie, skin, hair, top, legs, extras like beanie/sunhat/goggles/headphones/scarf, something held, and flags `core` for "A cozy few", `dozer`, `studier`), plus furniture (couch, armchair, cushion, beanbag, beach chair, towel, bench, stool, log, sleeping bag, train seat, café table, skis, surfboard…). Each room may set `scale` / `ax` / `ay` (the group grows from that floor point, default 1.2 from 560, 800); keep the right-most person left of the crate (x 808). `people.js` draws everyone facing left into `#people`. CSS hooks: heads bob with `.stage.is-on`, dozers close their eyes with Zs when `data-phase="idle"`, a "?" on error, the studier reads during `data-study="focus"` and has tea on breaks, every holiday hat is in each head and `data-hat` shows one (own hats hide), and scarves appear in winter. The café's own regulars (`.typist`, `.sipper`, `.cafe-orderer`) follow `data-crowd` too; the barista always works.
- `js/life-people.js` — lively people. A scheduler (every 6–11 s) plays one event: a **chat** (the left neighbour turns round with `.turned` on `.head-turn`; speech `.bubble`s with `.talking` mouths; lines from the room's `chats` plus context lines for a playing record, snow, rain, thunder, holidays, midnight), a **solo** moment (`.sip`, `.scroll`, `.type`, `.laugh`, `.stretch`, `.wave` animate the `.arm` / `.bodywrap`), a room **moment** (`MOMENTS`: popcorn, groove, warm, shiver, fan, water, snore, owl, window, type), a **snack run** (a `wanders` person's seat gets `.away` and their hidden standing copy `.walker` walks off right and back with a new `held()` item) or a **visitor** (built with `personSvg`, slotted in by `data-y`, walks in from the right along the front of the room (y 708) to just right of the crate (x 893) and out again; every 70–130 s; the café has no visitor, its customers come from `js/cafe.js`). `#people` comes after the cabinet and café counter in `index.html`, so walkers pass in front of them, never through. Walking tweens the SVG `transform` attribute with requestAnimationFrame. Blinks run on their own timer. Nothing runs with `data-lively="off"`, `data-crowd="empty"`, `data-phase="idle"` or reduced motion. Bubbles stay between the TV (x 380) and x 1000 in people-group units. `playEvent(kind, name)` triggers one for testing. **Smooth-motion rules:** lap items (laptop, popcorn, book; `isLapItem()` in `people.js`) are drawn in `.lap-hold` outside the arm so they never move with it, hand items in `.own-hold` on the arm; change what someone holds with `setHold(el, item)` (it also sets `data-hold`, which `solo()` uses: drinks sip, laptops type, popcorn munches, only empty hands stretch or wave). Arms have an elbow and a wrist (`.arm` at the shoulder, `.forearm` at the elbow, `.wrist` holding `.own-hold`), so a sip brings the cup to the mouth and tips it; heads (`.head-turn`, `.head`) turn and nod around the neck (view-box origin at x -0.5) so the neck stays on the shoulders. Only empty hands wave. Play a class-based moment with `act(el, cls)`, which waits for that person's animations to really finish before removing the class (no fixed waits that cut animations short). **Walking has knees:** the standing pose's legs are a `.thigh` (turning at the hip) holding a `.shin` and shoe (turning at the knee); `.stepping` runs the walk cycle in CSS (heel strike, push-off, swing with the knee bent; the far leg half a cycle behind; the body dips on `.bodywrap`; empty hands swing), and `.walking` sets the registered `--stride` to 1 so the stride eases in and out instead of snapping (remove `.walking` first, `.stepping` about 0.45 s later). Walks ease in and out (`walkTo`, theater `travel()`); sitting down and standing up crossfade the seated person with the `.walker` (`.out` then `.shown`, `.away` fades the seated one). Each room's `chats`, `moments`, `wanders`, `warms` and `visitor` are in the `LIFE` table at the end of `js/groups.js`; the train's conductor is its visitor.
- Wall art on the right wall (`.wall-art .art-<room>`, the café has its menu board instead).
- **Movie theater** (`data-room="theater"`): `.theater` is a stage-sized layer (z 20) drawn over the whole room, so nothing else needs hiding one by one; the TV screen (`.tv-set`, z 26) becomes the big screen (700 × 394 at x 290, y 92; 860 × 484 at x 210, y 40 with `data-seat="front"`), its TV body and knobs hide, and `.th-curtains` (z 27) sit above it. Curtains close only when `data-phase` is paused/idle, so the picture is never covered while it plays; house lights (`.th-house`) dim while playing; `#tv-idle` becomes a film countdown (`.leader`). The marquee shows the title (`#marquee-title`, set in `updateCard`). `fitPhone` uses `screenBox()` (the theater's screen on phones; curtains hide there and `.th-screen-glow` stands in). The screen plays the **Now showing** list (`showings` in `js/radio.js`, `record-room:showings:v1`, page "Now showing" in Decorate > Music; `intermission` = the main broadcast until videos are added) instead of the radio station: `theater` in `main.js` switches `musicSettingsChanged`, `nextOnDial`, `radioError`, the marquee (tap it for the next showing). Videos play one after another (state 0 → next). Records/crate are covered by the layer.
- `js/theater.js` — the audience from behind (`ROWS` per seat view; heads, caps, beanies, buckets, sodas; seeded so it's stable; 3D-glasses nights by date; `HOLIDAY_HATS` from `people.js`) and its events: laugh wave, munch (+ kernel flick "hey!"), whisper (+ "shh!"), phone glow, latecomer (late seats), usher, snack seller, screen shadow (only across the strip under the picture, y 486–518). Same rules as lively people; `playTheaterEvent(kind)` for testing.
- `js/decor.js` — the Decorate settings (`OPTIONS`, `DEFAULTS`), saved and applied to the stage; the panel itself is built by `js/panel.js`. Options are `choices` (on/off pairs become switches), `swatches`, `multi`, or `range` sliders (`volume`, `mixRain/Fire/Cafe/Purr`); `validate` allows dynamic values (your own stations). Returns `get`, `set`, `setMany` (presets and looks), `snapshot`, `refresh`, `close`. Applies choices as `data-room` (bedroom, cafe, cabin, beach, greenhouse, treehouse, train, theater), `data-crowd` (busy, cozy, empty), `data-lively` (on, off), `data-seat` (back, front; the theater), `data-rug`, `data-wallpaper`, `data-curtain`, `data-candles`, `data-fire`, `data-sky` (sunrise, golden, dusk, midnight), `data-weather` (rain, storm, snow, clear), `data-season` (spring, summer, autumn, winter — `seasonForDate()`, northern hemisphere), `data-holiday` (a space-separated list, so use `[data-holiday~="x"]` in CSS), `data-hat`, `data-pet`, `data-tank`, `data-lamp` and CSS variables (`--neon`, `--fur`…) on the stage. The main broadcast became the default station; `stationV2` in the saved settings moves visitors off the old `lofi` default once. Auto time follows the visitor's clock. Migrates the old `{ sky, rain }` settings. `lamp` isn't in the panel; the lamp itself is the switch.
- `js/panel.js` — the Decorate panel in an iOS Settings style: grouped lists (`SECTIONS`), rows that push a choice page with checkmarks, switches for on/off options, sliders, sub-pages (`{ sub, label, rows }`, e.g. Furniture) and custom pages drawn by other modules (`{ custom }`: presets, my looks, station, sleep timer — each `{ label, value(settings), build(scroll, { el, back, rebuild }) }`). Pages stack with a back button naming the previous page. On phones it's a bottom sheet.
- `js/ui.js` also has `actionSheet()` (Play Now / Play Next / Play Later, the study timer, the sleep chip).
- `js/sleep.js` — the sleep timer (this visit only): fades the music, switches the lamp off, the group dozes.
- `js/study.js` — the flip clock on the wall: shows the time; 25-minute focus and 5-minute break with `sfx.chime()`. Sets `data-study` (the room's studier reads, then has tea).
- `js/looks.js` — six presets and three saved looks (`record-room:looks:v1`); `PRESET_BASE` resets candles, fire and the crowd.
- `js/parallax.js` — the moving city: `--px` / `--py` on the glass, layers use the CSS `translate` property (adds to running animations). Mouse on desktop, tilt on phones (iPhone asks permission when the switch is turned on).
- Record queue (in `main.js`): the crate has Play Now / Play Later while a record plays; `queue` holds record objects; `putBack({ advance: true })` plays the next one, then the radio returns. The TV's right knob (`#tv-volume`) steps the volume.
- `js/ui.js` — `confirmDialog()`, an iOS-style alert used instead of `window.confirm`. Add a record and the crate are `.ios-sheet` dialogs; shared iOS styles are at the end of `css/style.css` (`--ios-*` variables, system font).
- **The window (Nov 2026, bigger):** glass 592 × 354 at x 344, y 52 (frame x 330–950, y 38–420; mullion x 625), curtains tied back at the sides (`clip-path` and an `::after` tie-back), rod at y 26, string lights swagged under it. Everything inside keeps the **old window's coordinates (0–512 × 0–322)** inside `.glass-in` (30 px in, 32 px down); the full-glass layers use `viewBox="-30 -32 592 354"` and shapes that ran edge to edge reach -30 and 562. The weather canvases and `.overcast` cover the whole glass. **Rain clouds** (`.clouds`: `.cloud-far`, `.cloud-near`, and in storms the lower `.cloud-storm` plus `.cloud-flash`, lit with the `lightning` timing) gather in rain, snow and storms; the moon and stars dim behind them and `.overcast` greys the view. The city view has a **street** under the elevated train (`.street`: pavement at y 295, lamp posts with `.street-glow`, `.puddles` that shine in the rain) and **people walking by** in `#passersby` (`js/outside.js`, tiny figures with coats, bags, a dog, and umbrellas that open in the rain). The train room's luggage rack sits above the window.
- `js/city.js` — draws the city as one SVG: buildings with window grids/bands and rooftop details, plus neon signs mounted on buildings (`BUILDINGS`, `BLADE`). Windows are left out behind signs, and signs avoid the window's middle bar (glass x 251–261), the moon and the branch.
- `js/weather.js` — rain (three depths, splashes), snow, lightning bolts (timed to the `.lightning` CSS flash) and drops on the glass, drawn on two canvases (`#weather-sky`, `#weather-glass`).
- `js/life.js` — pets wake when clicked and wander (the cat strolls the sill, the dog stretches or chases its tail, and trots to floor clicks, staying left of the crate; `.pet-stand` / `.pet-lying` poses crossfade with opacity; pets turn round with a narrowing `turn()` before walking, walk with eased WAAPI animations whose end state is written to the style before cancelling (`play()`), walk times are whole leg steps so legs stop straight, and they turn back to face left before lying down; `petNow('cat' | 'dog')` triggers one for testing, the dog's bed `.dog-cushion` is in the front-left corner), fish tank feeding, lamp switch, city windows switching on and off, seasonal extras.
- Candles (`#candles`, on the sill; on the counter in the café) and the fireplace (`#fireplace`, cabin and treehouse) light on click; `.flame-glow` warms the room, strongest at night.
- New rooms: greenhouse (glass walls, hanging planters, city view), treehouse (plank walls, garland and lantern, `.view-forest`), train (luggage rack, CAR 7, rounded window, `.view-train` with scrolling hills and poles; the whole car rocks together with `car-rock` on the stage, and `.room-controls` cancels it out with `car-steady` — never sway the people or seats on their own; the screen-edge slack in `fitStage` is 2px so the rocking shows no gaps).
- Home-screen app: `manifest.webmanifest`, `icons/` (drawn from `icons/icon.svg`; re-render the PNGs if it changes), and a full-screen button (`#fullscreen-toggle`) where supported.
- `js/sounds.js` — sound effects made with the Web Audio API (no files): `sfx.swoosh/crackle/click/bubbles/purr/thump/flick/thunder/chime/strike/puff`, plus the room-sounds mixer (`setAmbience()`: rain, fire, café murmur and purring beds made from filtered noise, on their own output so the effects switch doesn't mute them). Audio unlocks on the first click; thunder follows the `.lightning` CSS animation. Off via Decorate > Sound.
- Rooms: each room sets `--wall-*` / `--floor-*` variables and patterns, shows its `.room-prop`s and wall art, picks a window view (`.view-city`, `.view-mountains`, `.view-sea`, `.view-forest`, `.view-train`) and a group in `js/groups.js`. The TV, crate, pets and controls keep the same positions in every room.
- The café (`.cafe-only` elements): counter with pastry case over the cabinet, barista and espresso machine (the table lamp and mug hide), window bar with stools and two customers (seen from behind), pendant lamps (the light switch moves to them), a chalkboard menu, the neon sign on the wall above the menu board (keep it out of the window), and a pickup mat at the counter's left end. **A real café (Nov 2026):** five bistro tables (`bistro`) with bentwood chairs (`cafechair`), laid out in `CAFE_TABLES` / `CAFE_SEATS` in `js/groups.js`: one for two at the front left (in front of the TV console; the café moves the dog's bed to x 1040 by the counter), two front tables for two, and two little tables for one in the back row, set in the gaps between the front customers; customers sit in the `chair` pose (shorter thighs so knees don't meet under the table) facing their table or each other (`face: 'right'` mirrors a person or chair with `scale(-s, s)`), not the TV, and don't bob to the music. Each customer has `act` (type, phone, read, hold) and `drink` (`hot:<mug colour>`, `hotlatte:<colour>`, `iced`, or none); `tableThings()` in `people.js` draws the laptop, the drink and an optional `plate` on the table in the customer's own coordinates (table top at y -14). Hot drinks have rising `.wisps` of steam (not `.steam`, which the café hides). The barista (`.cafe-back`) has shoulder/elbow pivots (`.b-arm-l`, `.b-fore-l`, `.barista-arm`, `.b-fore-r`), a talking mouth (`.b-mouth-open`), a `.b-phone` and a `.b-cup` (hot or iced, pre-rotated so they're upright with the arm raised), and slides along behind the counter with `--bx` on `.cafe-back` (0 at the machine, -156px at the register end). `js/cafe.js` runs the café's life (only in the café, with Lively people on and no reduced motion): each seated customer loops through moves that fit their `act` and `drink` — `typing` bursts (forearm taps, screen flicker on `.table-things`), `scrolling` (thumb flicks), `page-turn` (the hidden `.page` sweeps across the upright book), `turned` glances, laughs, and sips. A hand-held drink (`act: 'hold'`) uses `sip` / `sip-iced` directly; a drink on the table (`.table-front`, at `DRINK_SPOT`) is fetched by `sipFromTable()`: lower the phone or book (pose class off, `.put-down` shown on the table), `reach`, swap the cup into the hand (`.lifted` hides the table one), sip, put it back, pick the phone or book up again. Every step checks `generation`, and `tidy()` puts everyone back (customers are found by their data, since pose classes come off mid-move). The barista's day is classes on `.cafe-back`: `b-phone`, `b-look-l` / `b-look-r`, `b-steam` (pitcher beside the machine under the wand), `b-shot` (espresso runs), `b-dip` (down to the ice well), `b-talk`, and the drink on the counter (`b-hot`, or `b-iced` then `b-filled` as the cold brew pours); `makeHot()` / `makeIced()` play a whole drink. In the café, `js/life-people.js` only runs chats (no solo moves) and skips `.cafe-busy` people; `cafe.js` skips people in its `lifeBusy` set. **Comings and goings (`js/cafe.js`):** customers are built at runtime (`personSvg` + `tableThings`, class `cafe-rt`, data in `cafe.js`'s own map, so `js/life-people.js` leaves them out). One walks in from the right (`DOOR_X`) on `LINE_Y` (feet at the counter's base), queues to the right of the register (`REGISTER_X`, the counter's left end), turns to face the counter (`turn()`, a narrowing flip), and posts a job; the barista (serving first, then clearing tables, then idling — phone, looking round, steaming — but breaking off as soon as someone's waiting) slides to the register, says hi (`say()` bubbles from `life-people.js`, `b-talk`), hears the order, slides back and makes it (`makeHot` / `makeIced` keeping the cup), carries it (`b-carry`), sets it down so it slides onto the pickup mat (`.pickup-cup`, `pick-hot` / `pick-iced` on `.cafe-counter`), and the customer reaches up (`reach-up`), takes it (`setHold`) and says thanks. With a seat reserved at arrival they `walk()` along the line and to the chair and crossfade into a seated customer (things appear on the table); with no free seat it's to go. Everyone (the opening customers too) leaves after a stay (1–4 min, by activity): they stand, walk out, and leave the empty cup (`.empty-cup`, no steam) until the barista comes out as a walking copy in an apron (`extras: ['apron']`), picks it up and goes back; a seat with a cup isn't free. Pace: Busy every 20–40 s up to 7 seated, A cozy few every 50–100 s up to 3, Nobody none; sometimes a pair (both seats at one table). Opening customers who leave just hide (`.gone`); `tidy()` removes the runtime ones and brings everything back. Test hooks: `cafeNow('arrive', { drink, act, toGo })`, `cafeNow('pair')`, `cafeNow('leave', seatId)`, `cafeNow('clear')`, `cafeNow('arrivals', false)`, `cafeNow('speed', n)`, `cafeNow('pause' | 'resume')`, and single moves `cafeNow(kind, pid)`.
- Seasons: the `.branch` outside the window, `.season-fall` (petals/leaves; faster in rain, blown in storms, hidden in snow), `.stuck` (on wet glass), `.fireflies` (summer clear nights), `.frost` (winter).
- `js/holidays.js` — the holiday calendar: `HOLIDAYS` (newyear, lunar, valentine, stpatricks, easter, july4, halloween, diwali, thanksgiving, hanukkah, winter), `holidaysForDate()` (several can be on at once), `hanukkahNight()` (menorah candles), `nextHoliday()` (the panel's countdown) and `HAT_FOR`. Lunar New Year, Diwali and Hanukkah use date tables for 2026–2030: add years before they run out. Easter and US Thanksgiving are calculated. Visitors choose which holidays to celebrate (`celebrate` setting); new holidays added later switch on for existing visitors (`celebrateKnown`).
- Holiday decorations live in `.holiday-garland` (rod), `.holiday-window`, `.holiday-shelf` (tree on the wall shelf, stockings on the TV console), `.holiday-sill`, `.ghost`, `.rainbow`, plus `.hat-*` in every person's head and `.pet-hat-*` on the pets.
- `js/records.js` — the record library, all in the crate: `loadLibrary()` (starters on a first visit; old shelf records from `record-room:records:v1` move into the crate once), `saveCrate()`, and `parseYouTubeId()` for watch, youtu.be, music, shorts, embed and live links.
- `js/covers.js` — iTunes cover lookup: searches songs and albums and picks the best artist/title match; `guessFromVideo()` reads "Artist - Song (Official Video)" titles; `fetchVideoInfo()` tries YouTube oEmbed to fill blank names; records still showing a thumbnail retry once they play (`upgradeCover()` in `main.js`, using the player's `videoData()`).
- `js/player.js` — YouTube IFrame API wrapper (load muted, mute/unMute, setVolume, `fade()`).
- `js/radio.js` — the radio `STATIONS` (the main broadcast `rFZHOHl-L8A`, whose title comes from the player's `videoData()`; Lofi Girl lofi hip hop, sleep, synthwave; Chillhop jazzy), your own stations (`record-room:stations:v1`, optionally tagged with a holiday) and `autoStation()` (jazzy in the morning, the main broadcast by day, synthwave in the evening, sleep at night; a tagged station on its holiday). Whenever no record is on, the TV plays the chosen station (`data-source="radio"` on the stage; `"record"` while a record is on). It starts muted because browsers block sound until a tap; the first tap anywhere fades the sound in (`enableSound()` in `main.js`). Picking a record fades the radio out; putting it back (or the record ending, which returns it after about 3 seconds) brings the radio back. The left TV knob (`#tv-knob`) changes station; a station that won't play is skipped. Settings: `radio` (on/off) and `station` in Decorate > Music. The station ids couldn't be checked from the build machine.
- Reduced motion: every animation has a still fallback in the `prefers-reduced-motion` block at the end of `css/style.css`.

Theater videos are stored under `record-room:showings:v1`. Records are stored per browser in `localStorage` under `record-room:crate:v1` (the old shelf key `record-room:records:v1` is migrated and removed), settings under `record-room:settings:v1`, your stations under `record-room:stations:v1`, saved looks under `record-room:looks:v1`.

Run locally: `python3 -m http.server 8000`, then open http://localhost:8000. YouTube won't play from a `file://` page.
