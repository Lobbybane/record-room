# Record Room — notes for Claude

Bobby's hobby project: a cozy lofi room you can play music in. A group of people (different for each room) hangs out watching the TV on the left wall, which plays the main broadcast (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rFZHOHl-L8A) as the room's radio. Records are the way to play other music: pick one from the crate and it floats to the turntable, the needle drops, and the song plays on the TV. Build in small steps and add features as we go.

Live site: https://lobbybane.github.io/record-room/ (GitHub Pages from `main`, root folder).

## Decisions already made

- **Look: lofi / anime night city.** Chosen over an earlier mid-century screen-print "park-service poster" version. Soft violet, pink and peach palette, a window onto a rainy city (neon signs, lit windows, elevated train), string lights, plants, a sleeping cat on the sill, a retro CRT TV on a console on the left wall, a lamp and a turntable on a cabinet. Grain and vignette on top. Keep new work in this style.
- **People, not a single listener (Nov 2026 redesign):** each room has a casual themed group facing the TV. No album covers on the walls (posters and record shelves were removed); records live in the crate. The listener's outfit/skin/activity settings were replaced by one `crowd` setting (busy / cozy / empty).
- **Music: YouTube's official IFrame Player API.** Free, full songs, no subscription. The player must stay visible (it lives on the TV screen). Never hide it, rip audio, download or re-host anything (no yt-dlp). Some uploads block embedding; show a friendly message and suggest another upload.
- **Covers: iTunes Search API** via JSONP, no key. Fallback is the YouTube thumbnail. Load covers at runtime, never save copies into the repo.
- **Ruled out for now:** Apple MusicKit (needs the $99/yr Apple Developer Program plus the listener's Apple Music subscription; Bobby has only a free developer account), Amazon Music (closed beta, approval and DRM required), Spotify (Premium plus tightened developer access). The player is written so another source could be swapped in later.
- **Stack:** plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript modules, no build step, no dependencies. Keep it that way unless there's a strong reason.

## How it works

- The scene is a fixed 1280 × 820 stage scaled to fit the window (`--s` CSS variable set in `js/main.js`). All positions are stage pixels.
- **The stage is fitted with CSS `zoom`, not `transform: scale` (Nov 2026 iPhone crash fix).** WebKit (every iPhone browser) paints a shrunk layer at its full size, so on a phone the room used ~1.8–2.3 GB and iOS closed the page ("A problem repeatedly occurred"); with `zoom` it's ~0.7 GB (measured in WebKitGTK's MiniBrowser at 3× pixels; a blank page is ~0.25 GB). `.stage` has `zoom: var(--s)` inside `@supports (zoom: 0.5)` (older browsers keep the `--fit: scale(--s)` transform); zoom also multiplies the stage's own `left`/`top`, so they're `calc(var(--cx) / var(--s))`. Anything that rebuilds the stage transform (the train's `car-rock`) uses `var(--fit)`, never `scale(var(--s))`. Turning screen points into stage pixels uses `--s` / `--cx` / `--cy` and the viewport (`stagePoint()` in `js/life.js`), not the stage's `getBoundingClientRect()`. Film grain is one small stitched-turbulence tile as a background (`.grain`), not a full-screen SVG filter. Before adding big full-screen layers, filters or blend modes, check phone memory.
- **Window view (Nov 2026; tap the glass, the W key; Esc, the "‹ Room" pill `#window-back` or another tap on the glass to come back):** `computeFit(view)` in `js/main.js` works a fit out as numbers (`{ layout, scale, cx, cy, cover, tv, box }`) and `applyFit()` sets the CSS variables, so `zoomTo(view)` can animate between two fits. Looking out, the glass (`GLASS`, x 344 y 52, 592 × 354) covers the screen on a computer (`pointer: fine`) and fits whole on phones and tablets (framed; less to draw); in the phone layout it fills the room's space at the top and the big TV stays below. **YouTube's player must stay visible**, so on wide screens the TV moves to the bottom-left corner (`--tv-t`, picture at least 206px tall, `.stage[data-view="window"] .tv-set`, antenna hidden). The zoom: the stage stays laid out as the room and is magnified by two Web Animations on the graphics chip (sampled keyframes of one camera path: scale changes evenly in ratio, the focus slides; the TV glides between its two spots), `.zooming` adds `will-change` and hides the corner buttons; at the end the close-up is laid out for real (sharp) and `cancel()`ed in the same frame. Never lay out close up during the zoom: drawing the whole room at 2–3× is what crashed iPhones. In the view, everything that isn't around the window is `visibility: hidden` (a keep-list in `css/style.css`; add new window-area layers to it), the corner buttons keep their room size (`--ctl`), and the grain keeps its on-screen size. WebKit memory looking out: ~0.94 GB phone upright, ~1.04 GB sideways (room ~0.7). A second tap mid-zoom turns round from where the camera is (`camera(currentTime)`). No window view in the theater or with Decorate open (opening it, or switching to the theater, drops back to the room); reduced motion switches at once. `stagePoint()`-style click maths (`--s`, `--cx`, `--cy`) decides whether a tap was on the glass, skipping buttons, pets and the TV.
- The whole room always stays visible. Spare screen space is filled by the wall, baseboard, floor, vignette and grain, which stretch to the screen edges using `--fx` / `--fy` / `--fw` / `--fh` (the screen's left, top, width and height in stage pixels). The stage centre is `--cx` / `--cy`. While the Decorate panel is open the room refits into the space beside it (or above it on phones). The Decorate button sits in the screen's top-right corner. The page is `position: fixed` so iPhone Safari's toolbar can't hide the bottom.

Walking tweens the SVG `transform` attribute with requestAnimationFrame. Blinks run on their own timer. Nothing runs with `data-lively="off"`, `data-crowd="empty"`, `data-phase="idle"` or reduced motion. Bubbles stay between the TV (x 380) and x 1000 in people-group units. `playEvent(kind, name)` triggers one for testing. **Smooth-motion rules:** lap items (laptop, popcorn, book; `isLapItem()` in `people.js`) are drawn in `.lap-hold` outside the arm so they never move with it, hand items in `.own-hold` on the arm; change what someone holds with `setHold(el, item)` (it also sets `data-hold`, which `solo()` uses: drinks sip, laptops type, popcorn munches, only empty hands stretch or wave). Arms have an elbow and a wrist (`.arm` at the shoulder, `.forearm` at the elbow, `.wrist` holding `.own-hold`), so a sip brings the cup to the mouth and tips it; heads (`.head-turn`, `.head`) turn and nod around the neck (view-box origin at x -0.5) so the neck stays on the shoulders. Only empty hands wave. Play a class-based moment with `act(el, cls)`, which waits for that person's animations to really finish before removing the class (no fixed waits that cut animations short), then `settle()`s for two frames. **Every move starts and ends in the person's own pose (Nov 2026):** move animations add on top of the pose (`animation-composition: add` on the moving parts, in an `@supports` block at the end of `css/style.css`, with relative keyframes such as `thumb-flick-add` / `pitcher-jiggle-add`), so keyframes must start and end at "no change"; the music bob runs on the separate `rotate` / `translate` properties of `.head` so moves on `transform` never cut it off (no `!important` head animations); a looping move is stopped with `endLoop(el, cls, targets)` (finishes the current cycle, then removes the class) and a barista pose with a loop on top with `stopPose()` in `cafe.js`; never remove an animation and set a new pose in the same instant (`settle()` between them, or the browser skips the transition); things change hands with `holdIn(el, item)` / `holdOut(el)` in `people.js` (fades, timed with the hand at the spot, crossfading with the table or counter item), and the barista's phone and cups fade (`opacity`, not `display`). The scratchpad `snaps.js` measured every move frame by frame for jumps. **Walking has knees:** the standing pose's legs are a `.thigh` (turning at the hip) holding a `.shin` and shoe (turning at the knee); `.stepping` runs the walk cycle in CSS (heel strike, push-off, swing with the knee bent; the far leg half a cycle behind; the body dips on `.bodywrap`; empty hands swing), and `.walking` sets the registered `--stride` to 1 so the stride eases in and out instead of snapping (remove `.walking` first, `.stepping` about 0.45 s later). Walks ease in and out (`walkTo`, theater `travel()`); sitting down and standing up crossfade the seated person with the `.walker` (`.out` then `.shown`, `.away` fades the seated one). Each room's `chats`, `moments`, `wanders`, `warms` and `visitor` are in the `LIFE` table at the end of `js/groups.js`; the train's conductor is its visitor.

A hand-held drink (`act: 'hold'`) uses `sip` / `sip-iced` directly; a drink on the table (`.table-front`, at `DRINK_SPOT`) is fetched by `sipFromTable()`: lower the phone or book (pose class off, `.put-down` shown on the table), `reach`, swap the cup into the hand (`.lifted` hides the table one), sip, put it back, pick the phone or book up again. Every step checks `generation`, and `tidy()` puts everyone back (customers are found by their data, since pose classes come off mid-move). The barista's day is classes on `.cafe-back`: `b-phone`, `b-look-l` / `b-look-r`, `b-steam` (pitcher beside the machine under the wand), `b-shot` (espresso runs), `b-dip` (down to the ice well), `b-talk`, and the drink on the counter (`b-hot`, or `b-iced` then `b-filled` as the cold brew pours); `makeHot()` / `makeIced()` play a whole drink. In the café, `js/life-people.js` only runs chats (no solo moves) and skips `.cafe-busy` people; `cafe.js` skips people in its `lifeBusy` set. **Comings and goings (`js/cafe.js`):** customers are built at runtime (`personSvg` + `tableThings`, class `cafe-rt`, data in `cafe.js`'s own map, so `js/life-people.js` leaves them out). One walks in from the right (`DOOR_X`) on `LINE_Y` (feet at the counter's base), queues to the right of the register (`REGISTER_X`, the counter's left end), turns to face the counter (`turn()`, a narrowing flip), and posts a job; the barista (serving first, then clearing tables, then idling — phone, looking round, steaming — but breaking off as soon as someone's waiting) slides to the register, says hi (`say()` bubbles from `life-people.js`, `b-talk`), hears the order, slides back and makes it (`makeHot` / `makeIced` keeping the cup), carries it (`b-carry`), sets it down so it slides onto the pickup mat (`.pickup-cup`, `pick-hot` / `pick-iced` on `.cafe-counter`), and the customer reaches up (`reach-up`), takes it (`setHold`) and says thanks. With a seat reserved at arrival they `walk()` along the line and to the chair and crossfade into a seated customer (things appear on the table); with no free seat it's to go. Everyone (the opening customers too) leaves after a stay (1–4 min, by activity): they stand, walk out, and leave the empty cup (`.empty-cup`, no steam) until the barista comes out as a walking copy in an apron (`extras: ['apron']`), picks it up and goes back; a seat with a cup isn't free. Pace: Busy every 20–40 s up to 7 seated, A cozy few every 50–100 s up to 3, Nobody none; sometimes a pair (both seats at one table). **The bus boy:** when cups pile up (Busy: 3+ tables with a cup, or 2+ while the barista is serving; A cozy few: 2+ while serving; never with Nobody), `busboyWatch()` sends him in from the kitchen off screen right (`BUSBOY` look: cap, darker apron `apron:#1F4F49`, a grey `tub` held at the hip — a lap item that shows up to 5 cleared cups, `tub:<n>`). He claims the cups on his route (`spot.claimed`, so the barista never goes for the same one; `dirtySpots()` skips claimed cups), visits the tables nearest the kitchen first, reaches, the cup fades into the tub, wipes the table with a `cloth` (`.wipe`, on `translate`), and walks back out; the barista sometimes says thanks. He rests at least 40 s between trips; the barista now only clears when fewer than 3 cups are waiting and he isn't out. `cafeNow('busboy')` sends him now. **Everyone walks in from off screen:** `offscreenX()` in `people.js` turns the screen's right edge (`--fx` + `--fw`) into people-group units for the current room plus a margin, so café customers, the bus boy, room visitors and snack runs start and end just past the edge on any screen size (no fade-in). Opening customers who leave just hide (`.gone`); `tidy()` removes the runtime ones and brings everything back. In CSS ("Time of day blends"): the sky gradient is painted on its own layer, `.glass::before` (`will-change`), from typed, non-inherited colour stops `--sky-a…e` / `--sky-pb…pd` set on `.glass::before` and `.moon-cut` (the crescent's cut shows the same piece of sky, so it matches any sky); the sea (`--sea-a/b`) and window glow (`--glow`) are typed too; shapes blend their own `fill` (buildings, roofs, far city, ridges, clouds, the lamp spill); `.night-sky` (stars, moon) and `.day-sky` (the sun) are wrappers that fade, and the sun glides (`left`/`top`: it rises from low left, sets behind the city at the right, and into the sea at the beach, where `.day-sky` is clipped at the horizon). **Never transition an inherited custom property on the stage**: it restyles the whole room every frame (8 fps on a slow phone); blends now run ~34 fps at 4× CPU throttle.

- `js/covers.js` — iTunes cover lookup: searches songs and albums and picks the best artist/title match; `guessFromVideo()` reads "Artist - Song (Official Video)" titles; `fetchVideoInfo()` tries YouTube oEmbed to fill blank names; records still showing a thumbnail retry once they play (`upgradeCover()` in `main.js`, using the player's `videoData()`).
- `js/player.js` — YouTube IFrame API wrapper (load muted, mute/unMute, setVolume, `fade()`). `loadYouTubeApi()` doesn't remember a failure (the next try asks again) and gives up after 20 s; `load()` waits at most 20 s for the player's `onReady` (`within()`), but the player keeps starting so a later try still works. In `main.js`, "No signal" retries when the phone comes back online, the page comes back into view, or the TV screen (`#tv-idle`) is tapped (`retryNoSignal()`). Sound unlocks on `touchend` / `click` / `keydown` (and `pointerdown` only for a mouse), because iPhone Safari only treats the end of a tap as permission to play sound.
- `js/radio.js` — the radio `STATIONS` (the main broadcast `rFZHOHl-L8A`, whose title comes from the player's `videoData()`; Lofi Girl lofi hip hop, sleep, synthwave; Chillhop jazzy), your own stations (`record-room:stations:v1`, optionally tagged with a holiday) and `autoStation()` (jazzy in the morning, the main broadcast by day, synthwave in the evening, sleep at night; a tagged station on its holiday). Whenever no record is on, the TV plays the chosen station (`data-source="radio"` on the stage; `"record"` while a record is on). It starts muted because browsers block sound until a tap; the first tap anywhere fades the sound in (`enableSound()` in `main.js`). Picking a record fades the radio out; putting it back (or the record ending, which returns it after about 3 seconds) brings the radio back. The left TV knob (`#tv-knob`) changes station; a station that won't play is skipped. Settings: `radio` (on/off) and `station` in Decorate > Music. The station ids couldn't be checked from the build machine.
- Reduced motion: every animation has a still fallback in the `prefers-reduced-motion` block at the end of `css/style.css`.

Theater videos are stored under `record-room:showings:v1`. Your café menu is stored under `record-room:menu:v1`. Records are stored per browser in `localStorage` under `record-room:crate:v1` (the old shelf key `record-room:records:v1` is migrated and removed), settings under `record-room:settings:v1`, your stations under `record-room:stations:v1`, saved looks under `record-room:looks:v1`.

Run locally: `python3 -m http.server 8000`, then open http://localhost:8000. YouTube won't play from a `file://` page.

## Known gaps

- Phone memory is checked with WebKitGTK (`apt-get install webkit2gtk-driver`, then MiniBrowser through `WebKitWebDriver` under `Xvfb` with `GDK_SCALE=3`, reading the web process's PSS); a real iPhone also holds the YouTube player in the same process.
- Playback was never tested from the build machine (it can't reach YouTube or its thumbnails); check it in a real browser after changes.
- The station ids in `js/radio.js` and the starter id in `js/records.js` couldn't be checked from the build machine; replace any that don't play.
- Room sounds are synthesized, so they're soft and simple; real recordings would sound better but mean hosting audio files.
- The home-screen icon and tilt permission need checking on a real iPhone.
- iPhone Safari may refuse to unmute the radio from a tap outside the video (sound now unlocks on the end of a tap, which should help; check on a real iPhone); then the TV's tag says "Tap the video to play" and YouTube's own button starts it.
